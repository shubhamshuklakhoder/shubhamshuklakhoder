## 🎯 Design Philosophy

### Why "ET"?
- **Memorable**: Simple two-letter acronym
- **Clear**: Instantly recognizable as "Expense Tracker"
- **Scalable**: Readable at any size
- **Professional**: No unnecessary decoration

### Why Text-Only?
- **Utility-First**: Focuses on function over form
- **Clean**: No visual clutter
- **Timeless**: Won't feel dated
- **Professional**: Serious tool for serious users
- **Versatile**: Works in any context

### Color Rationale:
- **Blue (#3B82F6)**: Trust, stability, professionalism
- **White Text**: Maximum contrast, excellent readability
- **Flat Design**: Modern, minimal, distraction-free

---

## 📐 Typography Details

**Font Family**: DejaVu Sans Bold
- Sans-serif for modern, clean look
- Bold weight for strong presence
- Excellent readability at small sizes
- Wide availability on Android systems

**Letter Spacing**: Default (tight, professional)
**Alignment**: Centered (balanced, stable)
**Weight**: Bold (900) - strong, confident

---

## 🎨 Brand Guidelines

### Do's ✅
- Keep "ET" centered on blue background
- Maintain white text color
- Use solid, flat colors only
- Keep design minimal and clean

### Don'ts ❌
- Don't add gradients or shadows
- Don't use icons or illustrations
- Don't change font to serif or decorative
- Don't alter letter spacing
- Don't add borders or frames
